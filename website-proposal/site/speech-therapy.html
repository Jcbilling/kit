Speech therapy

Billing for speech therapy practices.

On January 1, 2027, the main speech therapy treatment code is replaced by ten new timed codes. Here is what changes, and the errors that already get speech therapy claims denied.

Jan 1, 2027

92507 is replaced by ten new codes billed by time.

10

New codes: a base code for the first 30 minutes and an add-on for each 15 more, for five types of disorder.

$2,480

Medicare threshold for 2026, shared with physical therapy.

Common billing errors

What gets speech therapy claims denied.

More than one unit of an untimed code

Most speech therapy codes are billed once per day, no matter how long the session was.

How to avoid itBill one unit per day for untimed codes.

Missing GN

Every line has to say it’s speech therapy.

How to avoid itCheck every line before the claim goes out.

Billing 92522 and 92523 together

92523 already includes the speech sound evaluation of 92522.

How to avoid itChoose the one code that matches the evaluation done.

Forgetting the threshold is shared

Physical therapy the patient received that year counts toward the same $2,480.

How to avoid itAsk about other therapy at intake and track the combined total.

Not being ready for 2027

From January 1, Medicare stops accepting 92507. Private insurers decide on their own when they adopt the new codes.

How to avoid itDocument the minutes of every session now, and check each insurer’s date.

Codes we bill every day

We know the rule behind each code.

Which ones are billed by the minute, which only once per session, and which can’t go on the same claim.

  • 92507Per sessionIndividual treatment, until December 31, 2026
  • 92508Per sessionGroup treatment (stays in 2027)
  • 92521Per sessionEvaluation of fluency
  • 92522Per sessionEvaluation of speech sound production
  • 92523Per sessionEvaluation of speech sound production and language
  • 92524Per sessionBehavioral and qualitative analysis of voice and resonance
  • 92526Per sessionTreatment of swallowing and feeding
  • 92610Per sessionClinical evaluation of swallowing
Until Dec 31, 202692507

One code for all individual treatment, billed once per session.

→
From Jan 1, 202710 timed codes

A code for the first 30 minutes and an add-on for each 15 more, by type of disorder: fluency (92654, +92655), speech sound production (92656, +92657), language comprehension and expression (92658, +92659), combined speech sound and language (92660, +92661), voice, upper airway and resonance (92662, +92663).

What it means for you: start writing down the minutes of every session now. Medicare’s 2027 payment for these codes is still a proposal, and private insurers decide on their own when they adopt them.

Before a claim goes out

A quick check for every speech therapy claim.

  • GN on every line
  • One unit per untimed code
  • The right evaluation code, not two
  • KX if the combined total passed $2,480
  • From 2027: the right new code and the minutes documented
Sources

These are Medicare rules as of October 2026. Private insurers can have their own rules. We review this page every January, when Medicare updates them.
