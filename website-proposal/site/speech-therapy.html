Speech therapy

Billing for speech therapy practices.

On January 1, 2027, the main speech therapy treatment code is replaced by ten new codes billed by time. It’s the biggest change in speech therapy billing in years.

Where claims go wrong

What gets speech therapy claims denied.

Billing more than once for a single session

Most speech therapy services are billed once per day, no matter how long they last. Extra units are denied.

How we handle itWe bill each service the way Medicare counts it.

A claim line without the speech therapy requirement

Every line has to identify the service as speech therapy.

How we handle itWe check every line before it goes out.

Two evaluations that overlap

Some evaluations already include others, and billing both gets the claim denied.

How we handle itWe bill the one that matches the evaluation done.

Forgetting the limit is shared

Physical therapy the patient received that year counts toward the same limit.

How we handle itWe ask about other therapy at intake and track the combined total.

Not being ready for 2027

From January 1, the old code stops working for Medicare. Private insurers decide on their own when they switch.

How we handle itWe’re already preparing our clinics for the change, insurer by insurer.

Our expertise

Every code has its own rules. Knowing them is our job.

Which services are billed by the minute and which once per session, which ones can’t go on the same claim, and what every line needs to get paid. We keep track of it, and of every change Medicare makes each January, so you don’t have to.

  • Yearly limits tracked for every patient
  • Plans of care, signatures and reports followed up on time
  • Rule changes applied before they cost you

From Jan 1, 2027Ten codes, billed by time

What it means for you: the minutes of every session will matter. Medicare’s 2027 payment is still a proposal, and each private insurer decides when to switch. We’re following both for our clinics.

Our model

And we get paid only when you get paid.

If a claim is denied anyway, we correct it and follow it until it’s paid. You don’t pay a billing fee until then.
