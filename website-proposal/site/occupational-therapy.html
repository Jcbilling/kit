Occupational therapy

Occupational therapy billing for your clinic.

Occupational therapy has its own Medicare limit, its own evaluations and its own requirements on every claim. Here is where OT claims go wrong.

Where claims go wrong

What gets occupational therapy claims denied.

A claim line without the OT requirement

Every line has to identify the service as occupational therapy. One missing line can sink the claim.

How we handle itWe check every line before it goes out.

Mixing up the yearly limits

OT has its own limit. Applying the wrong one leads to denials or unnecessary extra steps.

How we handle itWe track the OT total for each patient on its own.

Re-evaluations billed as routine

Medicare pays for a re-evaluation only when there’s a documented reason.

How we handle itWe make sure the reason is in the note before we bill it.

Billing the wrong number of units

Timed care is billed by the total minutes of the day.

How we handle itWe check every claim against the minutes documented.

A plan of care signed late

Medicare requires a signed plan and periodic recertification.

How we handle itWe track the dates for every patient and follow up on signatures.

Assistant visits billed like therapist visits

Medicare pays them differently and checks them closely.

How we handle itWe record who provided each service and bill it correctly.

Our expertise

Every code has its own rules. Knowing them is our job.

Which services are billed by the minute and which once per session, which ones can’t go on the same claim, and what every line needs to get paid. We keep track of it, and of every change Medicare makes each January, so you don’t have to.

  • Yearly limits tracked for every patient
  • Plans of care, signatures and reports followed up on time
  • Rule changes applied before they cost you
