Occupational therapy

Billing for occupational therapy practices.

Occupational therapy has its own Medicare threshold and its own marks on every claim line. Missing one means a rejected claim.

What makes occupational therapy billing hard

The rules we work with every day.

Its own yearly Medicare threshold

$2,480 in 2026. Past it, claims without the modifier are denied.

How we handle itWe track each patient’s yearly total and add the modifier when it’s needed.

Every line of the claim has to say it’s occupational therapy

If the mark is missing, the claim is rejected.

How we handle itWe check every line before the claim is submitted.

Sessions by an assistant

They have to be marked and are paid at 85%.

How we handle itWe mark assistant sessions correctly before the claim goes out.

Sources: CMS Therapy Services; Medicare Claims Processing Manual, chapter 5. Checked October 12, 2026.
