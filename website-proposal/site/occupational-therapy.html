Occupational therapy

Billing for occupational therapy practices.

Occupational therapy has its own Medicare threshold, its own modifier and its own evaluation codes. Here is what gets OT claims denied, and how to avoid it.

$2,480

Medicare threshold for occupational therapy in 2026. It’s separate from physical and speech therapy.

30 days

For the physician or NPP to certify the plan of care.

10 days

At most, between progress reports (counted in treatment days).

Common billing errors

What gets occupational therapy claims denied.

Missing GO on a line

Every line has to say it’s occupational therapy. Without it, the claim is rejected.

How to avoid itCheck every line before the claim goes out, not just the first one.

Mixing up the thresholds

OT has its own $2,480. Applying the physical therapy total to an OT patient leads to a missing or unnecessary KX.

How to avoid itTrack the OT total separately for each patient.

Re-evaluations billed as routine

A re-evaluation is paid only when there’s a real change: new findings, a significant change in condition, or a patient who isn’t responding to the plan.

How to avoid itDocument the reason for the re-evaluation in the note.

Counting timed units wrong

Units come from the total minutes of all timed services that day.

How to avoid itWrite down the minutes of each timed service and add them up.

The plan of care isn’t certified on time

Signed within 30 days of the first visit and recertified at least every 90 days.

How to avoid itTrack the dates for every patient.

Assistant visits without CO

When an assistant provides the care, the claim needs CO, and it’s paid at 85%.

How to avoid itMark who provided each service in the note.

Before a claim goes out

A quick check for every occupational therapy claim.

  • GO on every line
  • Units match the minutes documented
  • Plan of care signed and current
  • Progress report within the last 10 treatment days
  • KX if the patient passed the OT threshold
  • CO if an assistant provided care
Sources
  • CMS, Therapy Services and Transmittal 13437 (2026 threshold)
  • Medicare Claims Processing Manual, chapter 5
  • Medicare Benefit Policy Manual, chapter 15
  • CMS, NCCI Policy Manual 2026, chapter XI

These are Medicare rules as of October 2026. Private insurers can have their own rules. We review this page every January, when Medicare updates them.
