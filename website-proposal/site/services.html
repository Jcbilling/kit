Core service

Insurance billing and claims

The full cycle for every claim, from before the visit to the payment in your account.

  • Eligibility verification for every patient, at every visit
  • Charge entry and claim submission
  • Payment posting and reconciliation
  • Follow-up on every unpaid claim
  • A monthly report for your practice
$7 per paid claim, or 5% of paymentsPricing details
Included in billing

Denials and appeals

A denied claim isn't a lost claim. We find out why, fix it and send it back.

  • Review of every denial and its reason
  • Corrections, resubmissions and appeals
  • Follow-up with the insurer until it's resolved
  • Patterns in your denials, and how to prevent them
$0 extra. You pay only when it's paid.
Before you bill

Credentialing

Getting you enrolled with the insurers your patients use, so you can bill from day one.

  • Applications with Medicare, Medicaid and private insurers
  • Ongoing CAQH and payer enrollment maintenance
  • A clear timeline, with updates along the way
$500 per entity, 3 months, then $20/hour

  • Review of your unpaid and aging balances
  • Follow-up on claims that can still be recovered
  • A plan so it doesn't happen again

Getting started

How switching to JCB works.

  1. A call with Shannon

    Twenty minutes to understand your practice and see if we're a good fit.

  2. Review

    We check your credentialing and map every open claim, so nothing gets lost.

  3. Both billers, side by side

    Your current biller finishes the claims in progress. We take the new ones from an agreed date.

  4. Handover

    You approve the plan and the documentation, and we take over fully.

Decision 8: how long this takes in total. Our materials say two to four weeks in some places and three to four in others.
