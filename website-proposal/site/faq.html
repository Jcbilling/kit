Questions clinics ask us

Straight answers before you book a call.

Getting started

Do I have to change my EHR?

In most cases, no. Connecting with the major systems is part of our standard setup. We fit into the way your practice already works.

I already have a biller. What happens to the claims in progress?

We first review where every open claim stands. Your current biller finishes the claims already in progress, and we take the new ones from an agreed date, so no claim is left without someone in charge. You see the plan and approve it before anything moves.

How soon can you start?

Decision 8: two to four weeks, or three to four.

Do you only work with therapy practices?

Physical, occupational and speech therapy, and behavioral health are our specialty. If they're most of what you bill, other services at lower volume are not a problem.

Pricing

What happens when a claim is denied?

We correct it, resubmit it or appeal it, and follow it until it's resolved. You don't pay a billing fee on that claim until it's paid.

Can a new practice afford it?

For new and small practices, we can defer the setup fee for up to a year, and the monthly maintenance can be adjusted at the start. The billing fee only applies when a claim gets paid.

What does the monthly maintenance cover?

The HIPAA-compliant platform, its security, the connections to the clearinghouses and insurers, and the updates that keep every claim in the format each insurer requires.

How does credentialing work?

$500 per entity for the first three months, then $20 per hour. You always know what it costs before we start.

Working together

What will I see each month?

A report for your practice with your collection rate, your denials, your unpaid balances by age and how each insurer is paying. And you can ask us anything between reports.

How long is the contract? Is there a trial period?

Decision 8: month-to-month or one year, and whether we offer an initial period of 60 or 90 days.

If we stop working together, do I get my data?

Yes. Your data is yours. You receive the complete records of every claim, payment, denial, appeal and patient account, ready for whoever comes next.
