Pricing

What it costs. Everything, before you sign.

Most billing companies charge for every claim they touch. We charge only when a claim is paid, and every fee is on this page.

Setup
$2,000
one time

Can be deferred for up to a year for new and small practices.

Maintenance
$500
per month

Can be adjusted in the early stage, case by case.

Billing, you choose
$7
per paid claim

Or 5% of payments received, or a combination. We model which one costs you less.

Credentialing
$500
per entity, 3 months

Then $20 per hour once the process is in motion.

Optional: documentation support, $50 per patient · EMRFlow app, $100 per provider.

Decision 1: whether we publish the full pricing. Also to confirm: how the combination works, whether the $100 for EMRFlow is per month, and whether we still pass on postage and storage costs.
Why per paid claim

A denied claim costs you nothing.

With a traditional biller, you pay when the claim is submitted, and often again when it's resubmitted. With us, the billing fee appears once: when the money reaches your account.

Other companies charge a flat monthly fee whether your claims get paid or not. Our fee only grows when your collections do.

Partner clinics

Who we work best with.

We are growing our network of partner clinics, and we choose them carefully.

We work best with practices that are

  • Independent and owner-operated, where the owner is part of day-to-day decisions
  • Physical, occupational or speech therapy, or behavioral health
  • Ready for a billing partner who is accountable and in touch
  • Looking for long-term alignment over quick wins

We are not the right fit if you

  • Are part of a large network or hospital system
  • Are looking for the lowest possible price above everything else
  • Want someone to "just handle it" without communication
