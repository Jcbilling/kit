About us

Built for therapists. Led by someone who knows billing from the inside.

PhotoProfessional portrait of Jennita Coles
JC Billing and Management

Jennita Coles started JC Billing and Management after years of watching dedicated therapists and healthcare workers struggle to get paid for their work, often while serving the people who need care the most. She had spent years billing for many kinds of providers and every type of insurance, and she knew most of those payments were being lost to denials, missing paperwork and claims nobody followed up on.

Today JCB handles billing, credentialing and claim follow-up for physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy and psychology practices. Our work is 100% remote, so we support practices anywhere in the United States. We work with Medicare, Medicaid and private insurers such as BlueCross BlueShield and UnitedHealthcare.

Our four commitments

Jennita built a team around one idea: the people who care for patients shouldn't have to chase their own payments.

1

Our success is tied to yours.

We get paid only when you get paid.

2

You understand what you pay before committing.

We explain our pricing upfront, with no surprises.

3

We follow every claim until it's resolved.

Denied claims are corrected and resubmitted, not forgotten.

4

We're accountable for our work.

You always know where your claims stand and who is handling them.
