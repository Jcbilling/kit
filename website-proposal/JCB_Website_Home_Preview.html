Our four commitments

Four things we operate by, every day.

1

Our success is tied to your success.

Our fees are tied to what you actually collect. If a claim is denied or delayed, we don't charge a billing fee until it's resolved.

2

You understand what you're paying, before committing.

Every fee is explained before anything is signed: setup, maintenance, per claim, credentialing and optional services.

3

We follow through on every claim.

Submitting a claim isn't the same as getting paid. We pursue every denial, underpayment and aging balance until the money lands.

4

We're fully accountable. Partner, not vendor.

When something breaks down, we bring a solution, not an excuse. Our business only grows when yours does.

Questions clinics ask us
Do I have to change my EHR?

No. We work with the systems you already use. Our EMRFlow app is optional.

What happens when a claim is denied?

We correct it, appeal it and follow it until it's resolved. You don't pay a billing fee on that claim until it's paid.

We're a new practice. Can we afford the setup?

The $2,000 setup fee can be deferred for up to a year, and maintenance may be waived early on, case by case.

How does credentialing work?

It's $500 per entity for the first 3 months, then $20 per hour once the process is in motion. You'll know what's included before we start.
